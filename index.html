FAQ

Good to know

Is the food very spicy?

Some dishes like suya and peppered chicken have a proper kick, but we're happy to go easy on the heat. Just ask when you order.

Can I pre-order for collection?

Yes! Send us your order via the form, WhatsApp or a phone call and we'll have it ready for your chosen time during opening hours.

Do you cater for parties and events?

We do. We prepare party trays of jollof rice, peppered meats, suya, meat pies and puff puff. Give us as much notice as you can, especially for large numbers.

I've never tried Nigerian food. What should I get?

Start with jollof rice and chicken suya, then finish with a bag of puff puff. It's the perfect introduction.

Where is parking?

We're inside Macclesfield Market Hall in the Grosvenor Centre, with town-centre car parks a short walk away.
